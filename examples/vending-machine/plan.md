# Build Plan: vending-machine

## Tests

- [ ] Buy with 1 emerald: emerald leaves inventory, 1 bread arrives, stock 10 -> 9.
- [ ] Buy with 0 emeralds: nothing happens, stock unchanged, no error message.
- [ ] Buy at stock 0: no payment, 'Sold out' appears.
- [ ] Two players next to the machine: only the nearest one is charged per press.
- [ ] Press restock: stock returns to 10 and the action bar confirms.
- [ ] Drop an emerald after pressing: the clear step must fail and the bread must not be given.

## Design notes

- The conditional pipeline is the all-or-nothing guarantee: stock gate -> clear -> give -> stock decrement.
- cleanup and sold_out are unconditional so they run even when a conditional step failed.
- c=1 is intentional; remove it only if you want every nearby player to buy at once, and then fix stock accounting.
